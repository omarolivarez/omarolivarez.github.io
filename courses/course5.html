Empirical Methods II

Harvard Masters Econometrics Course

This 5-week module equips students with an understanding of common tools of quantitative empirical analysis in policy applications. The course covers describing relationships between multiple variables, handling data in the real world, the challenges surrounding making causal claims, and interpreting the results of studies in a way that is both technically sound and policy relevant. The course focuses primarily on the tool of regression analysis and deepen students' facility with computer software widely used in analyzing quantitative data.

Course details

  • Level: Masters
  • Professor: Dr. Soroush Saghafian
  • Term: Spring I 2025
  • Class size: 45
  • Course assistant rating: 5.0/5.0

Course objectives

  • to learn to interpret regression estimates
  • to devellop skills related to causal inference and prediction
  • to practice statistical analysis in R using quasi-experimental techniques
